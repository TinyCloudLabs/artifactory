# Synthesis Report

Build an on-demand assessment across the selected authorized context. Preserve
agreement, uncertainty, and conflicting interpretations instead of flattening
them into false consensus.

Quality bar:
- state the central assessment and why it matters
- connect every finding to authorized evidence
- express uncertainty explicitly
- preserve material dissent or state that none was found
- publish distinct Feed posts that target relevant report sections

Invalid outputs:
- a source-by-source recap with no synthesis
- hidden disagreement or unsupported certainty
- a post without a section target and evidence

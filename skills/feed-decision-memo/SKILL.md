# Decision Memo

Frame a pending decision from selected authorized context. Make the choice,
options, tradeoffs, evidence, and unanswered questions legible without choosing
on the reader's behalf.

Quality bar:
- state the decision to be made
- present at least two genuinely different options
- make tradeoffs explicit for every option
- connect evidence to each option
- end with questions or conditions that could change the decision

Invalid outputs:
- a recommendation disguised as the only option
- options without tradeoffs or evidence
- generic advice untethered from authorized context

# Short Insights

Turn only newly authorized transcript content into a durable insight collection and
several concise Feed posts. Each post must stand on its own, express a distinct
idea, and cite the source evidence that supports it.

Quality bar:
- ignore content already covered by the workflow cursor
- require a clear claim and implication for every insight
- publish two or more non-duplicative, evidence-backed posts
- preserve the full insight collection as the expandable artifact

Invalid outputs:
- a post without source evidence
- repeated paraphrases presented as different insights
- an insight that cannot be traced to the authorized source window

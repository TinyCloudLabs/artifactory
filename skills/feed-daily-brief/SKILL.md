# Daily Brief

Compress the latest authorized context into a role-aware daily brief. Prioritize
what the reader needs to know, explain why it matters, and carry forward relevant
follow-ups from earlier briefs.

Quality bar:
- rank developments by reader relevance
- distinguish evidence from implications
- state confidence explicitly
- thread a follow-up only when the selected source window restates it; prior
  artifact titles and fingerprints are dedupe hints, not historical evidence
- derive each Feed post from a specific brief section with evidence

Invalid outputs:
- an undifferentiated transcript summary
- unsupported confidence or certainty
- a post that cannot expand to a named section of the artifact

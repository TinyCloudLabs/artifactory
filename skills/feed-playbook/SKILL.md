# Playbook

Turn a concrete process described in authorized conversations into a practical,
repeatable playbook. This starter demonstrates that Feed is not limited to
intelligence-style reports: operational knowledge can become durable content and
concise, actionable posts too.

Quality bar:
- name the outcome, prerequisites, owner, and ordered steps
- retain cautions and validation checks from the source
- ground each step in authorized conversation evidence
- publish distinct posts for useful steps, cautions, or checks

Invalid outputs:
- a generic process invented beyond the transcript
- unordered advice with no owner or completion check
- a post that cannot expand to a specific playbook section
